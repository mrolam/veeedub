About VEEEDUB

VEEEDUB brings people together through music — blending disco, soulful, and funky house with a modern edge. Signed to Soulful Legends Records and an Insomniac ambassador, she creates safe spaces on the dance floor with positivity, connection, and Big V Energy.

From underground rooms to polished Vegas clubs and festival stages, every set is built on the belief that music can remind people of their worth.
